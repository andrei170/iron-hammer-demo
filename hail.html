What hail actually does to a roof

  • Bruising of the mat under the granules, which does not show from the driveway
  • Granule loss, which shortens the roof’s life without leaking today
  • Cracked and split shingles, often on one slope only
  • Damage to vents, flashing, and soft metals - frequently the clearest evidence an adjuster will accept
  • Gutters and downspouts, which are checked because they date the storm

Why the timing matters

🔴 Most policies limit how long after a storm you can file. A roof that was hit two seasons ago and never inspected is frequently a roof that can no longer be claimed. ⭐ The inspection is free, so there is no reason to find out late.
