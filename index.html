Why do this before you call your insurer?

The order matters more than almost anything else in a storm claim.

A claim filed without evidence is a claim that gets underpaid

An adjuster has twenty minutes and a checklist. If nobody has documented the damage properly beforehand, the scope that gets approved is whatever was spotted in those twenty minutes.

Not every storm is a claim, and we will say so

⭐ Filing a claim that gets denied still goes on your record. If the damage does not support a claim we tell you that at the inspection, free, rather than filing and hoping.

You pay the deductible, not the roof

Where a claim is approved for replacement, your cost is the deductible. We also offer financing, and we will beat a written quote from the last 30 days by $250.
