What a supplement is, and why it decides the job

⭐⭐ An adjuster’s first scope is frequently incomplete — it misses ridge, flashing, drip edge, code upgrades or a second layer. A supplement is the formal request to correct it, and most homeowners do not know it exists. It is the difference between a repair allowance and a replacement.

What it costs you

Where a claim is approved for replacement, your cost is your deductible. Financing is available. ⚠ We will not offer to waive, absorb or rebate a deductible, because in most states that is insurance fraud and anybody offering it is telling you something about themselves.
